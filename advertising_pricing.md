# TexasBusiness.News Advertising Pricing

Effective date: August 22, 2026  
Status: Advertiser preview rate card  
Contact: admin@texasbusiness.news

## Overview

TexasBusiness.News offers clearly labeled advertising beside constructive Texas business coverage. Inventory is designed for county, regional, industry, section, and statewide campaigns without disguising paid placements as editorial content.

This rate card follows the fixed package prices, creative formats, annual discount, and additional-county rules used by The County Post advertiser preview, tailored to a Texas-only publication.

Pricing is not based on county population.

Stripe checkout is not active in this preview. Campaign requests are reviewed before inventory is confirmed, creative is accepted, or payment is collected.

## Additional Counties

Eligible additional or adjacent counties are available at 50% of the base package price.

- Preferred additional county: $47.50 monthly or $475 annually.
- Gold additional county: $147.50 monthly or $1,475 annually.
- Platinum additional county: $247.50 monthly or $2,475 annually.
- County Advertiser additional county: $497.50 monthly or $4,975 annually.

The first county is purchased at the full package price. Each additional county must use the same advertiser, placement type, creative set, and campaign period. Geographic eligibility and adjacency are confirmed during review.

## Discounts, Premiums, And Add-Ons

### Multi-Placement County Bundle

- A 10% package discount may apply when an advertiser purchases three or more eligible placement elements in one county.
- The discount must be included in the approved insertion order.

### Category Exclusivity

- Add 25%–50% to the applicable package price.
- Availability depends on geography, industry, campaign timing, and existing advertiser commitments.

### Extra Feed Sponsorship

- Gold: add $50 per month for an additional feed beyond the base tier inclusion.
- Platinum: add $100 per month for an additional feed beyond the base tier inclusion.

### Reporting

- Future reporting add-on: $25–$100 per month when campaign analytics are available.
- Current browser-side impression and click events are directional and do not constitute guaranteed delivery.

### Creative Production

- A one-time setup fee may apply if TexasBusiness.News creates or materially rebuilds advertiser artwork.
- Production work begins only after scope and price are approved in writing.

### Founding Advertiser Availability

- TexasBusiness.News may limit founding advertiser packages to three to five advertisers per county.
- Availability is not guaranteed until confirmed.

## Creative Specifications

### Square And Card Creative

- Canvas: 250×250 px.
- File type: JPG or PNG.
- Maximum intake size: 10 MB.
- Recommended delivered size: under 300 KB.
- Use concise, high-contrast copy that remains legible on mobile.

### Page-Bottom Banner

- Canvas: 980×300 px.
- File type: JPG or PNG.
- Maximum intake size: 10 MB.
- Recommended delivered size: under 500 KB.
- Keep essential text and logos inside a centered safe area so responsive scaling does not impair readability.

### Destination Requirements

- Provide one valid HTTPS destination URL.
- Redirects and tracking parameters are reviewed before activation.
- The destination must accurately represent the advertiser and the offer shown in the creative.

## In-Feed Placement Behavior

- In-feed inventory is inserted after every five editorial article cards in each feed section.
- The placement spans the available feed grid and uses responsive minimum and maximum sizing.
- Spacing and visual treatment preserve separation between adjacent articles.
- Placeholder inventory is visibly labeled as available advertising space.
- Live campaigns must remain visually distinct from editorial cards and carry a sponsored or advertisement disclosure.

## Submission And Review

The advertiser reservation flow collects:

- Placement and billing selection.
- Selected Texas counties when the placement supports county targeting.
- Business and contact names.
- Contact email.
- Destination website.
- Optional JPG or PNG creative selected for local preview validation.
- Campaign timing, region, industry, and other notes.

On the preview branch, the creative file is not uploaded. The request records only the local filename, and TexasBusiness.News follows up for final delivery through an approved method.

Requests are sent to admin@texasbusiness.news through the configured EmailJS contact service. Submission does not guarantee availability, acceptance, pricing, or publication.

## Checkout Status

Stripe checkout is intentionally deferred.

The interface includes a disabled checkout stage to communicate the planned flow, but:

- No card details are requested.
- No Stripe SDK is loaded.
- No checkout session is created.
- No payment is collected.
- No inventory is automatically reserved.

A future implementation should create checkout sessions through a secure backend endpoint. Stripe secret keys must never be included in the Vite frontend or AWS Amplify client environment.

## Measurement

Live placements may emit:

- Ad impression events after at least 50% of the creative enters the viewport.
- Ad click events.
- Campaign, creative, slot, county, and region metadata where applicable.

Measurement is directional and may be affected by browser privacy settings, blockers, connection failures, or consent requirements. TexasBusiness.News does not guarantee a specific number of impressions or clicks unless a signed insertion order explicitly states a delivery commitment and remedy.

## Advertising Standards

TexasBusiness.News may reject or request changes to advertising that:

- Is deceptive, materially inaccurate, or unsupported.
- Mimics editorial content or omits required disclosures.
- Promotes unlawful products, discriminatory practices, malware, or unsafe destinations.
- Uses another party’s trademarks, likeness, copyrighted work, or personal information without authorization.
- Conflicts with applicable FTC requirements or Texas law.
- Creates unacceptable performance, accessibility, or readability problems.

Health, financial, legal, insurance, political, alcohol, gambling, firearms, hunting, and other regulated or sensitive categories may require additional review, disclosures, targeting restrictions, or rejection.

## Privacy And Compliance

- Advertiser requests should contain business contact information only.
- Do not submit sensitive personal information, payment card data, government identifiers, health information, or account credentials.
- Any future behavioral targeting, tracking, or audience enrichment must be reviewed against the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act and other applicable laws.
- Email outreach and lead follow-up must follow applicable CAN-SPAM requirements.
- Sponsored placements must remain identifiable under FTC advertising disclosure principles.

## Rate And Inventory Changes

- Rates may change before a campaign is confirmed.
- Confirmed insertion orders control if they conflict with this preview document.
- Inventory is subject to editorial layout, technical availability, advertiser-category conflicts, and exclusivity commitments.
- Custom packages, creative production, takeovers, or placements not listed here require a written quote.
